# Syncora: Release Notes & System Overview (v1.0.0)
**Stakeholder Summary & Platform Capabilities**

## 1. Executive Summary
Syncora is an AI-native, multi-tenant institutional operating system designed to unify academic core, finance, security, and student life into a single, seamless platform. Built on a rock-solid foundation of multi-tenant isolation and granular RBAC, Syncora provides institutional leadership with unprecedented oversight while delivering modern, high-fidelity experiences to students and faculty.

---

## 2. Platform Architecture & Security
*   **Multi-Tenant Substrate:** Absolute data isolation at the database layer ensuring no cross-institution data leaks.
*   **Auth & JWT Strategy:** Stateless JWT-based authentication with stateful session management and rotated refresh tokens.
*   **Granular RBAC:** A precise Role-Based Access Control matrix managing permissions across all 16+ core modules.
*   **Developer Extensibility:** A robust API Documentation Portal and Interactive Sandbox for third-party integrations and custom development.

---

## 3. Core Modules & Capabilities

### Academic Management
*   **Institutional Hub:** High-level dashboard for academic oversight and organizational management.
*   **Timetable & Attendance Engine:** Smart scheduling with proactive conflict detection and real-time attendance tracking via mobile.
*   **Faculty Gradebook:** Data-driven assessment tools with granular grade management and performance analytics.
*   **Programs, Sections & Rooms:** Full administrative control over the academic physical and logical substrate.

### Student Experience
*   **Digital Identity:** Interactive, glassmorphic digital ID cards with 3D flip functionality for campus-wide access.
*   **Student Dashboard:** A "Today at a Glance" command center for schedules, assignments, and wellness.
*   **Public Onboarding:** A streamlined entry flow including institution search, OTP verification, and first-time setup.
*   **Help Center & Support:** A comprehensive support portal with integrated documentation and ticket tracking.

### Finance & Operations
*   **Billing & Invoicing:** Complete financial lifecycle management with automated invoice generation and mobile payment flows.
*   **Scholarship Management:** End-to-end workflow from fund allocation to committee review and award tracking.
*   **Late Fee Automation:** Logical rule-builder for automated penalty triggers with transparent audit summaries.
*   **Fleet & Logistics:** Real-time tracking of institutional transport and campus logistical assets.

### Safety & Governance
*   **Security Command Center:** Real-time campus monitoring with active incident logs and broadcast alert controls.
*   **Emergency Management:** Dedicated dashboard for crisis response and gate operations (Guard View).
*   **Platform Settings:** Global governance for security policies, institutional branding, and feature toggles.
*   **Audit & Analytics:** Comprehensive organizational reporting engine providing deep insights into institutional health.
