code_blocks Rule Summary

IF Student has outstanding Tuition Fees OR Hostel Dues

AND days past due date > 5 (Grace Period)

THEN Apply Initial Penalty of $25.00

AND Accrue $2.50 per day

UNTIL Total Penalty reaches $100.00 cap or balance is paid.

Example Scenario

Due Date: Oct 1
Payment Date: Oct 10
Days Late: 9 days
Total Late Fee: $35.00
($25 initial + (4 days * $2.50))
